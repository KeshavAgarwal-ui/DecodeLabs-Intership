Story

Built by two roasters and one stubborn thermometer.

We started Kindling in a garage with a 1kg sample roaster, logging every batch by hand because the difference between "good" and "great" coffee is usually a matter of seconds. That habit never left — every bag you get today is roasted from the same logged profile that won it a place in our lineup.
